2. Eligibility & Team Formation Rules
Team Size: Minimum 2 members, maximum 4 members per team. Solo participants are not permitted to submit projects independently and must use the team-matching portal before Day 1, 12:00 PM IST.
Cross-Track Participation: A team can submit a project to only one main track, but is eligible for all general sponsor prize bounties.
Participant ID: All participants must carry official student IDs and keep their registration QR badge visible at the on-site venue.

3. Tracks, Themes, and Problem Domains
Track 1: AI & Autonomous Agents: Building LLM systems, multi-agent frameworks, RAG workflows, or task automation tooling.
Track 2: Web3, Fintech & Decentralized Apps: Smart contracts, decentralized identity, payments, and blockchain security.
Track 3: Healthcare & MedTech: Diagnostics tooling, patient management, accessible health tech, and AI medical triage.
Track 4: Open Innovation & Smart Campus: Logistics, smart city solutions, IoT, sustainability, and open-source public goods.
Track 5: Gen AI

4. Code of Conduct & Academic Integrity
Originality Policy: All project code, design assets, and workflows must be created during the official 48-hour hackathon window.
Pre-existing Code & Open-Source: Third-party open-source libraries, UI components (e.g., Tailwind, Shadcn), APIs, and pre-trained public models (e.g., Hugging Face, OpenAI API) are fully allowed. However, pre-built proprietary applications or repos with commit histories before Day 1 at 10:00 AM IST are strictly disqualified.
Use of Generative AI: Using GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, and similar code generation tools is permitted for prototyping. Teams must disclose AI assistance in their project README.md.
Behavior Policy: Zero tolerance for harassment, offensive language, intellectual property theft, or disruptive behavior.

6. Submission Audit & Technical Requirements
All teams must submit before Day 3 at 10:00 AM IST on the submission portal with the following four deliverables:
1. Public Repository URL: GitHub/GitLab link containing clear setup steps, MIT/Apache 2.0 license, and an updated README.md.
2. Demo Video Link: YouTube or Loom URL (Maximum 3 minutes long, publicly viewable, showcasing working features).
3. Live Deployment / APK Link: Working web URL (e.g., Vercel, Render) or downloadable binary/APK.
4. Presentation Deck: PDF slide deck (Maximum 7 slides covering: Problem, Solution, Architecture, Tech Stack, Business Impact).
Note: Submissions missing a public repo link or exceeding the 3-minute video limit will fail automated compliance validation.
